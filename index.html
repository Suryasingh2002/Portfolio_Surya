About Me

Design systems
with a purpose

I'm Surya Pratap Singh, a UI/UX Designer based in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. With an MCA background and hands-on front-end development experience, I transitioned into design focusing on Agentic UX — interfaces that think alongside users.

My process starts with systems thinking. Every component, flow, and interaction serves a coherent whole. I work end-to-end in Figma — from user research and wireframing through to high-fidelity prototypes ready for developer handoff.

Currently exploring how AI-driven interfaces can be made more human — intuitive, anticipatory, and emotionally resonant.
